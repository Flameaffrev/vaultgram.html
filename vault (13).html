Activation Flow

Dead by default. Alive on signal.

The vault follows a zero-trust model. Every access attempt is verified, timestamped, and logged. The tunnel signal is the only key, and it expires in 60 seconds.

Security Model

Four walls of verification.

Every tunnel signal must pass four independent security checks before the vault even considers opening. A single failure keeps the vault sealed.

HMAC Signature

Signals are signed with HMAC-SHA256. Any tampering invalidates the signature instantly.

Timestamp Window

Signals older than 60 seconds are rejected automatically. Prevents replay attacks.

Nonce Uniqueness

Every nonce is burned after first use. Consumed nonces are remembered, even across page reloads.

Rate Limiting

Each tunnel ID is limited to 5 failed attempts per minute. Excess triggers lockout.

Tool Registry

Twelve slots. Yours to configure.

Register up to twelve callable tools that only open while the vault is open. Each tool runs in its own frame, and your signal's clearance level decides which ones you can open. VaultGram opens like any other tool once the vault is open. At Level 5 you can post and publish; at lower levels you see the posts the owner published.

When launch.html transmits a signal, the vault listens via storage events. It verifies HMAC, timestamp, nonce, and rate limits. On success, the sealed overlay is removed and the tool slots allowed by your clearance level become accessible.

Quick Start

Wire it up in minutes.

Open both files in the same browser. Transmit a signal from Launch. The vault unlocks automatically when a valid signal is detected.

Usage
# Step 1: Open vault.html in your browser
# Step 2: Open launch.html in another tab (same browser)
# Step 3: Select tunnel ID and clearance, then click Transmit
# Step 4: vault.html detects the signal, verifies it, and unlocks

# To re-seal the vault, click "Manual Seal" or close and reopen the page
